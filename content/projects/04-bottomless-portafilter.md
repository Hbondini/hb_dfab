## 1. Concept & Design
Designed to fit the **Breville 54mm** system, this portafilter is machined from a **single piece of aluminium** (monoblock construction) to eliminate the weak point found in standard two-piece press-fit designs. 

![Initial Design Sketch](/images/coffee-portafilter/porta-filter-sketch.jpg)
*Initial concept sketch exploring the handle geometry.*

---

## 2. Prototyping
Before cutting metal, the geometry was validated using 3D printed PLA prototypes to ensure ergonomic fit and locking engagement with the group head.

![3D Printed Prototype](/images/coffee-portafilter/3d-printed-prototype.jpg)

### Testing & Validation
The final prototype undergoing fit testing to ensure perfect engagement with the group head.

![Prototype Testing](/images/coffee-portafilter/prototype-testing.jpg)

---

## 3. Machining Process
The complex internal geometry required multiple setups. Note that the lugs seen protruding from the handle in the photos are temporary features, designed specifically for workholding and orientation during the milling process.
